\caption{Three ways for $K$ parties holding different images of the same entities to train a classifier with a label owner. (a)~Split learning trains each party's encoder from the label owner's gradients, so activations go up and gradients come down at every mini-batch. (b)~FedOnce~\cite{wu2022fedonce} uploads once, but each party must first train its encoder on its own unlabelled data. (c)~Our protocol keeps the single upload and replaces the party-trained encoder by a frozen public foundation encoder, optionally followed by a PCA fitted by each party alone (dashed); the label owner then trains a head for as long as it wants. Orange: encoders trained by the parties; blue: frozen public encoder; green, dashed: optional PCA.}\label{fig:schemes}
